About

I'm a software engineer at Oracle in Redwood Shores, where I own the scalar-quantized IVF vector indexes behind Oracle AI Vector Search. I work on how vectors are compressed, built into indexes and rescored at query time, and I follow new quantization research like TurboQuant and RaBitQ to see what belongs in future IVF and HNSW indexes.

Before this I did my MS in Computer Science at Purdue, where I worked on reinforcement learning for LLM reasoning. I've also interned at AWS on Redshift, spent two years on storage features and backend services at Oracle in India, and studied electrical engineering at IIT Delhi, where my first paper was about training neural networks on analog hardware.
